# KRI Collection Process Note

## Rules and conditions

As per the bank's process note, KRI information from different departments is to be collected by sending an email to each department on the first day of each Nepali month at 10:00 AM.

A follow-up email is to be sent after 3 working days if the required information has not been received from the intended persons.

The deadline for submission is within 13 working days of the month.

## Department collection frequency

- **Information Technology (IT):** Monthly submission using the designed template.
- **Digital Banking (DG):** Monthly submission using the designed template.
- **Human Resources (HR):** KRI collection every Nepali quarter. The email is to be sent on the first day of the quarter-end month at 10:00 AM; reminders are to be sent every 3 days. The deadline is within 13 working days of the succeeding month after each quarter end.

## Application requirement recorded in the source

Design an application to collect and analyze KRIs, map each KRI to approved thresholds, display results as Green, Amber, or Red, prepare a report, and escalate to the Operational Risk Management Department (ORMD) for now.
